    How to play Tide Market

    • 🏪 Buy goods from the market row — click a card (or press 1–4). You pay its price in coins.
    • 📈 Prices move with the tide. Each morning a tide event shifts every good's multiplier. Buy when a good is cheap, sell when it is dear.
    • 🖐️ Sell cards from your hand — click to select, click again (or press S) to sell at the day's price.
    • ◆●▲■ Sets pay: hold 3+ cards of one good and every sale of that good pays ×1.5.
    • ⛵ Brine trades too. Watch the harbor log: he buys cheap and sells dear. Beat him to the bargains.
    • 🏆 After 8 days the richer trader — purse plus hand value — rules the harbor.
